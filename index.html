Our Story

A taste of India, served with soul.

From a small kitchen in New Delhi to the seaside of Grimstad, our family has spent three generations perfecting the art of regional Indian cooking. Every dish on our menu is built on hand-ground masalas, slow-cooked gravies and the kind of hospitality that only comes from cooking for those you love.

We source the freshest Norwegian produce and seafood, and pair it with traditional spices flown weekly from Kerala, so every plate is a quiet bridge between two worlds.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Where is Namaste Express located?
We are at Torvet 5, 4876 Grimstad, Norway, right in the centre of Grimstad, a short walk from the harbour.
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Saturday we are open 16:00–21:30, and on Sunday 14:00–21:30.
Can I order takeaway online?
Yes. Use the Order Online page to build your order, choose a pickup time and select how you want to pay at the counter: cash, card or Vipps.
Do you take table reservations?
Yes, you can book a table online through our reservation page, or call us on 939 99 333.
Do you offer vegetarian and halal dishes?
Absolutely. We serve a wide range of vegetarian dishes such as Palak Paneer and Vegetable Korma, and our meat is halal.
Do you cater for parties and events?
Yes, we offer catering for private parties, company events and celebrations in Grimstad and the wider Agder region. Call us to plan a menu.
